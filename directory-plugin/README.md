# Hermoso for Claude

Launch and market from your coding agent: put the site you built live with a domain, email and payments, then
research ads, make image and video ads, post and schedule, and run campaigns. Requires a Hermoso account (free to
start).

Hermoso takes an idea from your own AI agent to launch and growth. Launch puts the site your AI writes live on
Hermoso hosting, connects or buys a domain, gives you email on that domain, and takes payments. Then market it:
research the ads competitors run, make on-brand image and video ads, post and schedule to your own social channels,
and run ad campaigns on 13 ad platforms, with analytics to see what works.

Send any image or video ad to Canva to edit, and bring finished Canva designs back to post, schedule or run as ads.

**Start free: 160+ free credits, no card. No markup on models.** Plans at
[hermoso.ai/pricing](https://hermoso.ai/pricing).

## What is in this plugin

- **The Hermoso MCP server**, connected remotely at `https://app.hermoso.ai/mcp`. Nothing is installed on your
  machine and no key is stored: the first time a Hermoso tool runs you sign in with your Hermoso account in the
  browser (in Claude Code, `/mcp` and then `hermoso`).
- **Seven skills** that teach Claude how to use it well:

| Skill | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| `hermoso-launch-app` | Put what you built live (hosting, domain, email, payments), then launch ads and a week of posts |
| `hermoso-research` | Find competitors and pull their real running ads, then the hooks worth copying |
| `hermoso-ad-from-brand` | One finished on-brand ad from a website or a short description |
| `hermoso-generate` | Images, videos and multi-scene videos from a prompt |
| `hermoso-product-photoshoot` | Studio, lifestyle and marketplace product shots from a real product photo |
| `hermoso-marketing` | The whole loop: research, create, publish and schedule, paid campaigns, results |
| `hermoso-edit-timeline` | The manual for custom video edits and transitions |

## Ask in your own words

- "Publish this folder as my site, then connect my domain and set up hello@ on it."
- "Pull the longest-running Meta ads for allbirds.com and tell me the three angles they keep paying for."
- "Make a 15-second UGC video ad for our product in 9:16."
- "Schedule this video to TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts for Tuesday at 9am."
- "Build a Meta Ads campaign for this video with the daily budget I give you, leave it paused, and show me the
  preview."

Nothing is published, bought, scheduled or spent on ads unless you say so. Ad campaigns are created paused.
